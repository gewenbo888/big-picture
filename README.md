# The Big Picture — An Analytical Companion · 《大图景》深度解读

An independent, bilingual (English / 中文) analytical companion to Sean Carroll's **«The Big Picture: On the Origins of Life, Meaning, and the Universe Itself»** (© 2016 Sean Carroll). It maps the book's argument into ten themes and rebuilds them as original interactive visualizations: poetic naturalism (one world, many true vocabularies), Bayesian reasoning (the flagship "Planets of Belief" credence updater), the Core Theory of everyday physics, the arrow of time, emergence and layers of reality, the origin of complexity and life, consciousness, free will, and the construction of meaning.

This site is **independent commentary and analysis — not the book**, and not affiliated with the author or publisher. The science is held firmly and explained in our own words; the boldest, genuinely contested philosophical moves (the deflation of consciousness, compatibilist free will, constructed meaning) are flagged as contested, with dissenting voices kept at the table.

## Stack

Next.js 14 (App Router) · React 18 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS. All visualizations are hand-built Canvas / SVG with zero chart dependencies (a working Bayesian updater, an entropy sim, a scale-zoom across layers of reality). Fully bilingual with a no-reload language toggle. A warm "poetic-naturalist cosmos" palette on deep navy, Space Grotesk + Spectral typography.
